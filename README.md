# ironveil-support# Iron Veil — Support

Iron Veil is a turn-based tactics campaign for iPhone. Thirteen kingdoms, 93 scenarios, no energy timers and no subscriptions.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports and feedback: **sherwood_mousavi@hotmail.com**

I read everything. If you are reporting a bug, it helps enormously to include your device model, your iOS version, and which scenario you were on.

## Common questions

### Is the whole game free?
Yes. Every kingdom and all 93 scenarios are playable without paying. The game is supported by ads. A one-time $2.99 purchase removes them permanently — it does not unlock content, because there is no locked content.

### I bought Remove Ads and the ads came back.
Open the menu and tap **Restore Purchases**. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID rather than to the app, so it survives reinstalling, changing device, or starting a new save. If it does not come back, email me with the Apple ID region you purchased in.

### Does it need an internet connection?
No. The campaign plays entirely offline. A connection is only used to load ads and to check your purchase.

### Where are my saves kept?
On your device. There are multiple save slots and the game keeps a backup, but deleting the app deletes your progress. Your ad-free purchase is not affected.

### Is there a way to play without ads for free?
Ads appear between battles, never during one, and never after a defeat. There are no ads at all in the tutorials or the first kingdom. Rewarded videos are always optional — nothing in the campaign is gated behind one.
