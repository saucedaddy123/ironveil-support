Iron Veil — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 5 September 2026
The short version
Iron Veil has no accounts and no login. I do not collect your name, email
address, or any personal information, and I have no server that stores anything
about you. Your save data stays on your device.
The game shows ads, and the advertising service does collect an identifier from
your device. That is the only data collection in the app, and this page explains
it.
What the app itself collects
Nothing. Iron Veil stores your campaign progress locally on your device. It is
never uploaded anywhere and I have no way to see it.
Advertising
Iron Veil uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and process:
	•	your device’s advertising identifier
	•	advertising data, such as which ads were shown and interacted with
	•	product interaction data, such as app launches
This information is used to select and measure ads, including ads based on your
activity in other apps and websites. It is not linked to your identity, because
Iron Veil holds no identity to link it to.
Google’s handling of this data is governed by their own policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
Your choices
Tracking. iOS asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be
used for tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised.
You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
Removing ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase stops all advertising in the
app, and with it the data collection described above.
Purchases
The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment
details. Apple tells the app only whether the unlock is owned by your Apple ID.
Children
Iron Veil is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect
information from them.
Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
